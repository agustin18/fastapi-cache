Add a read-only Dependabot delta mode to the existing reconciler and a manual/scheduled workflow. The delta reports new, reopened, fixed, dismissed, changed and missing alerts without modifying snapshots or triage files. Exit codes distinguish security-blocking findings (`1`), incomplete/unknown data (`2`) and material changes on open high/critical alerts that require explicit review (`3`); the 21 unchanged accepted open alerts do not require reapproval. The workflow uses the read-only `vulnerability-alerts` permission and publishes a run summary.
